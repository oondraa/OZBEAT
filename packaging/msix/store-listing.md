## English

**Short description**

Your music on the big screen: cover art, lyrics word by word and visuals that move with the music.

**Description**

OZBEAT fills your screen with whatever is playing. Play music the way you normally do, on Spotify, YouTube in the browser or any app in the Windows media controls, or on BluOS speakers, and OZBEAT shows it:

• Lyrics word by word, the moment each one is sung, even for many songs Spotify has no lyrics for
• The blurred album cover flowing behind everything, in the song's own colors
• Visuals that move with the sound
• Just the lyrics, floating over the desktop and every app (Ctrl+Alt+T)
• Two screens: the cover on your monitor, the lyrics big on the TV (Ctrl+D)
• Buttons to pause, skip and change the volume
• WLED lights in the colors of the cover

No account, no ads.

**Features** (one per line)

Lyrics word by word, in time with the song
Cover art as a flowing background
Works with Spotify, browsers and any app in the Windows media controls
BluOS speakers found automatically
Lyrics alone over the desktop and every app
Two screens: cover here, lyrics on the TV
Pause, skip and volume from the screen
WLED lights in the cover's colors

**Keywords**

music visualizer, lyrics, Spotify, synced lyrics, karaoke, album art, BluOS

Žádný účet, žádné reklamy.
